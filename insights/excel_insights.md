# Excel Analysis Insights

## Overview

This Excel analysis was used as the first exploratory step of the Profit Leak Analysis project.

The goal was to identify which categories, sub-categories and regions were responsible for weak profitability.

## Key Metrics

- Total Sales: €12.64M
- Total Profit: €1.47M
- Overall Profit Margin: 11.61%

## Key Findings

1. The company generated €12.64M in sales and €1.47M in profit, with an overall profit margin of 11.61%.

2. Furniture is the weakest category, with a profit margin of only 6.94%, compared with 13.69% for Office Supplies and 13.99% for Technology.

3. Tables are the clearest profit leak. They generated €757k in sales but produced a loss of €64k, resulting in a negative profit margin of -8.46%.

4. Tables also have the highest average discount rate within Furniture, at 29.07%, compared with the Furniture average of 16.80%.

5. Tables are not unprofitable in every region. The largest losses are concentrated in Southeastern Asia, Western Europe, South America, Eastern US and Southern Europe.

## Business Recommendation

The company should review its pricing and discount strategy for Tables, especially in regions where discounts are high and margins are negative.

Reducing unnecessary discounts or adjusting prices in the worst-performing regions could improve overall profitability without needing to increase total sales.
