# Business Insights & Recommendations

## Executive Summary

The Global Superstore analysis covers 2014–2017 and identifies profitability weaknesses despite sustained revenue growth.

Total sales reached approximately 12.64M, generating 1.47M in profit and an overall margin of 11.61%. Although absolute profit increased, the margin declined from 11.95% in 2016 to 11.73% in 2017.

The primary area of concern is the Tables sub-category, where high discount levels are associated with substantial losses.

## Key Findings

### 1. Tables is the main loss-making sub-category

Tables generated approximately 757K in sales but recorded a net loss of 64K, equivalent to a −8.46% profit margin.

### 2. Higher discounts are associated with weaker profitability

For Tables, profit margins vary substantially by discount range:

| Discount Range | Profit Margin |
|---|---:|
| No discount | +23.09% |
| 1–20% | +4.97% |
| 21–40% | −18.09% |
| Above 40% | −76.73% |

### 3. Losses are concentrated in specific regions

The largest absolute losses from Tables occurred in:

- Southeastern Asia: −18.6K
- Western Europe: −15.0K
- South America: −13.4K

Southern Europe recorded the highest average discount on Tables (53.16%).


## Business Recommendations

1. **Review discount policies:** Prioritize Tables transactions with discounts above 20%, assessing their profitability and potential impact on sales volume.

2. **Investigate regional losses:** Examine pricing, shipping costs and product mix in Southeastern Asia, Western Europe and South America.

3. **Review loss-making products:** Prioritize the ten Table products with the largest accumulated losses for detailed cost and pricing analysis.

4. **Monitor profitability alongside revenue:** Track sales, profit margin, discount levels and return indicators regularly rather than focusing exclusively on revenue growth.

5. **Test potential improvements:** Evaluate pricing and discount changes through controlled pilots before implementing company-wide policies.
